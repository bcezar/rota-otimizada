Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

This policy explains what data Find My Route collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

1. Data we collect

We collect: your email (for login and communication); the addresses you type in to optimize routes (geocoded via Google Maps or OpenStreetMap); usage data and analytics cookies (Google Analytics), when you consent to them; and your IP address, used only to limit abuse (rate limiting).

2. How we use your data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: calculating routes, saving your favorite routes, managing your Pro subscription, and improving the product experience. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-party services involved

  • Google Maps — address geocoding and distance calculation
  • Stripe — payment processing for the Pro subscription (we never receive or store card data)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (login, notifications)
  • Turso — the database where your account and saved routes are stored
  • Google Analytics — usage statistics, only with your consent via the cookie banner

4. Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also revoke analytics cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser data.

5. Security

Payment data never touches our servers — your card is processed directly by Stripe, a certified payment gateway. There are no passwords in our system: login happens via a magic link sent to your email.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us on our contact page or on Instagram @rota.otimizada.
