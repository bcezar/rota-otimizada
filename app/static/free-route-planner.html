Why do other tools charge?

Tools like RouteXL, Circuit, and OptimoRoute charge monthly fees because they add enterprise features such as real-time tracking, driver signature capture, and integration with management systems.

The free plan covers routes of up to 5 stops (+ starting point and final destination) — ideal for occasional use. For independent drivers, small businesses, and sales reps with larger itineraries, the Pro plan raises the limit to 50 stops per route.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use Find My Route immediately, with no email, no password, and no credit card. Your address history is saved automatically in your browser.

If you want to save routes to access from another device, just create a free account with your email.

Does it work offline?

Find My Route needs an internet connection to geocode addresses and calculate real distances. But during route execution, after opening Google Maps or Waze, those apps may work offline depending on the downloaded maps.
