How Find My Route Optimizes Your Route

Find My Route uses computational intelligence to calculate the visit sequence that minimizes the total distance traveled. Here's how it works under the hood.

The Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP)

The challenge of visiting multiple addresses in the most efficient order is called the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP). It is one of the most studied problems in computer science.

For 10 addresses, there are more than 180,000 possible sequences. For 20 addresses, that number exceeds 1.2 quintillion. Testing all combinations manually is impossible.

How Find My Route solves this

1
Geocoding — Each address you type is converted into geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) via Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.
2
Distance matrix — We calculate the real road distance between every pair of addresses using the OSRM API (Open Source Routing Machine).
3
Optimization with OR-Tools — Google OR-Tools' TSP algorithm finds the lowest-cost sequence in seconds, using GLS (Guided Local Search) metaheuristics.
4
Practical result — You receive the ordered list with distance and estimated time per leg, plus a direct link to Google Maps or Waze with the route already built.

Starting point and final destination

You can (optionally) define a starting point — such as your warehouse or work address — and a fixed final destination. The algorithm respects these constraints and optimizes only the intermediate stops.

You can also mark any stop in the list as "first destination" or "last destination", forcing its position in the route without removing the others.

How many addresses can I use?

The free plan supports up to 5 stops per route (+ starting point and final destination). The Pro plan expands this limit to 50 stops, ideal for longer delivery routes. Calculation time is approximately 2 to 5 seconds for 50 stops.

Is it free?

Yes. Without an account, you can make up to 5 optimizations per day, with up to 5 stops per route. A free account gives you unlimited daily optimizations. Find My Route has no intrusive ads.
