1. What the Service is

Find My Route is a route-planning tool that calculates the most efficient visiting order across multiple addresses, using geocoding and real road-distance data from third-party providers (Google Maps, OpenStreetMap).

2. Plans and limits

The free plan lets you optimize routes with up to 5 stops at a time, no sign-up required. The Pro plan raises that limit to up to 50 stops per route and is billed as a recurring monthly subscription.
