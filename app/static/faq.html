Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to your questions about how to use Find My Route to optimize delivery routes, sales visits, and any trip with multiple addresses.

Is Find My Route really free?
The free plan allows you to optimize routes with up to 5 stops (+ starting point and final destination), with no mandatory registration and no daily route limit. The Pro plan raises this limit to 50 stops per route.
How many addresses can I add per route?
On the free plan, up to 5 stops per route (plus starting point and final destination). On the Pro plan, up to 50 stops per route — enough for the vast majority of delivery routes and sales visits.
Do I need to install any application?
No. Find My Route works directly in your phone or computer browser, with no installation required. Access it at findmyroute.com.br.
How is the route opened in Google Maps?
After optimizing, click "Open in Google Maps". The app opens automatically with all points already inserted in the calculated order. On mobile, you can use Execution Mode to navigate stop by stop.
Does it work with Waze?
Yes. In Execution Mode, each stop has an "Open in Waze" button that takes you directly to the current address. Find My Route remembers your preference so it doesn't ask every time.
Can I import an address spreadsheet?
Yes. Find My Route accepts CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files. The file must have an "address" column and, optionally, a "description" column. You can download a sample template directly from the app.
Is the route calculated with real road distances or straight-line distances?
With real road distances, using the OSRM API (Open Source Routing Machine). This ensures the algorithm considers available roads, not just the straight geographic distance.
Can I define where I want to start and end?
Yes. You can define a "Starting point" (e.g., your warehouse) and a "Final destination" (e.g., return to warehouse). The algorithm will respect these fixed points and optimize only the intermediate stops.
Can I share the route with someone else?
Yes. After optimizing, click "Share" or "Copy link". A short link is generated and can be sent via WhatsApp, email, or any messenger. The recipient will see the same list of addresses.
What is Execution Mode?
It is a focused screen for use in the field. It shows one stop at a time with the full address, distance and estimated time, navigation buttons (Maps or Waze), and options to mark as visited, skip, or add a note. You advance to the next stop with the "Next →" button.
Can I save routes to use in the future?
Yes. Create a free account with your email and you can save routes with a name and access them from any device under "My Routes".
Does Find My Route work in rural areas / small towns?
Yes. The system uses Google Maps to geocode addresses and OpenStreetMap as a fallback. Coverage is good across virtually all regions, including smaller towns.
How do I cancel my Pro subscription?
Cancel anytime, with a single click, right from "My Account" — no need to contact anyone. When you cancel, your Pro access stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
Is it safe to pay on Find My Route?
Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, a certified payment gateway — Find My Route never receives or stores your card data. Pro access is unlocked automatically as soon as payment is confirmed.
How do I get in touch if I have a question?
Use the "Contact us" section inside the app (☰ menu) or reach us on Instagram @rota.otimizada.
